# Ideas for embedded view extensions

We think there are tree main opportunities for generating ideas for extensions.
- Integrating existing software
- Building new feature based on user demand
- Solving your own or your organizations needs

To help facilitate the second we would encourage you to check out the [Forma User Forum](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/forma-forum/bd-p/6140) and espcesially our post on [extensions](https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/forma-forum/analysis-extensions-in-forma-get-involved/td-p/12052087). You can find ideas there and interact with our users directly. You can also post your ideas there to solicit early feedback.
