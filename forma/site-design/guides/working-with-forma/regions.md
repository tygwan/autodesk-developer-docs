# Regions

Forma exists in multiple regions, with segmentation on a Hub level. Each region exists under its own top-level domain (
app.autodeskforma.eu or app.autodeskforma.com), and thus each region must be treated as an entirely separate entity.

Resource identifiers will not be of any use without knowing what region they exist in. Unless otherwise specified you
may assume that an API/service is region0specific.

For managing regions when building extensions please refer to the TOS. Please note that it is important to clearly
communicate to your users the geographic location their data might reside in.
